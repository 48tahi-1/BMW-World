THE BMW STORY

Bayerische Motoren Werke, widely known as BMW, is a German automobile and motorcycle manufacturer known for its engineering, premium design and driving performance.

Founded in 1916, BMW developed from an engineering company into one of the world's recognised premium automotive brands. Its vehicles combine technology, comfort, innovation and performance.

From elegant sedans and versatile SUVs to high-performance M models and fully electric vehicles, BMW continues to develop different ways to experience driving.
